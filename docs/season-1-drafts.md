# Season 1 drafts (2026-09-14; part 2 of each arc is the pitch Shirley chose from nine by DeepSeek, written out by hand)

Drafts for Shirley to veto before anything is seeded. The plan: every man gets a three-part arc.
Part 1 is free (exists). Part 2 is Plus. Part 3 opens after part 2 and is MATURE (web build
only), so it is Plus by inheritance. Same format as the seed: a brief is written to the actor,
never to the reader; options are the reader's intents, with where they go and what they credit;
photos are cards, existing where one fits, marked NEW ART where one has to be made.

What exists today: Ash "Four minutes" (7 beats), Ash "What he does not know" (3 beats, MATURE),
Rafe "The forty-second floor" (5 beats), Jun "Sixty floors of nothing" (7 beats).
